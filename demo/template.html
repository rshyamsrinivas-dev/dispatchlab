01 / What the agent sees

Current location, time left, loaded parcels and waiting orders. It never sees future arrivals.

02 / What it learns

A small linear Q-function scores waiting, returning, and feasible delivery stops. The weights are learned through simulated experience.

03 / What this leaves out

Traffic, uncertain travel times, multiple vehicles, charging, service times, and real geography. Reward points are not money.
