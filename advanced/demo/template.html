01 / What changed

Joint control of two vehicles, uncertain travel durations, battery reserve checks, charging, priority parcels, and service time at every stop.

02 / How the agent learns

Dueling Double DQN with replay memory, a target network, and masked actions. Reactive rules guide some exploration during training; evaluation uses only the neural policy.

03 / What the result means

The planner is a serious comparator, not an optimal fleet solver. All demand is synthetic. Better simulation results do not establish real-world operational value.
